## JarvisLabs — research note (2026-11-18)

Pages read: https://jarvislabs.ai/termsandservice (Terms of Service, undated; its markdown source at /termsandservice/page.md renders the body client-side), https://jarvislabs.ai/products/token-factory (Token Factory product page, pricing table and FAQ), https://jarvislabs.ai/contactus (offices). `GET https://models.jarvislabs.net/v1/models` answers `401 A valid bearer token is required`, so the API catalog itself is not public.

- ONE LEGAL PAGE: jarvislabs.ai publishes no standalone privacy policy. The Terms of Service carries a section titled "Privacy Policy, Information Collection, Storage and Use Policy" and that section is the privacy policy. `/privacy`, `/privacypolicy`, `/policies/privacy` and `/dpa` all 404, and `sitemap-0.xml` (128 URLs) lists only `/termsandservice` among legal pages. Both `sourceUrl` and `privacyUrl` therefore point at the same URL.
- TRAINING: off, stated for the API only. "Do you store my prompts or train on them? No. We don’t store your prompts or completions, and we don’t train on them." (Token Factory FAQ; the same sentence appears in the "Data and billing" section). The Terms of Service never address inference inputs or outputs and make no training claim either way, so this is product documentation, not a contractual term.
- RETENTION: not stored, stated for the API. "We serve each request on our own GPUs and keep no copy of it" and "We don’t store your prompts or completions" (Token Factory page). Cached input bills automatically "with no cache-write fee". The Terms of Service conflict by silence and by reservation: "We reserve the right to retain all information provided for as long as the information is needed" and "Following termination of your user account, Jarvislabs.ai may retain your private profile information for a commercially reasonable time for backup, archival, or audit purposes." That clause sits in the privacy section about account information, but it is the only retention statement the binding document makes.
- ZDR: yes for Token Factory content, default, nothing to configure ("Prompts aren’t stored"). No request-level or account-level switch is documented because retention is claimed to be absent by default. Zero retention is not stated anywhere in the Terms.

Registered office: Awfis, First Floor, A-24/9, Mohan Cooperative Industrial Estate, Mathura Road, Saidabad, New Delhi 110044. - COMPLIANCE: no DPA published, no SOC 2 or BAA claimed, no subprocessor list. Contact is engineering@jarvislabs.ai.
- STATUS/AI TOOLS: no public status page — `status.jarvislabs.ai` does not resolve, and `jarvislabs.statuspage.io` serves Atlassian's own generic status-page marketing site, not Jarvislabs' incidents.
- INCIDENTS: none found.
- Confidence: medium. The product page is explicit and specific about prompts, completions and training, and its India-region statement is unambiguous; the binding Terms, however, never mention inference content and reserve indefinite retention of "all information provided", so the strongest protections rest on marketing copy.
- Rating: **Guarded**. Zero retention and no training are stated for the API, which is a Clean-shaped posture, but the only contractual document is silent on inference content and reserves indefinite retention, and the provider publishes no DPA, SOC 2 or status page. That is "generally safe with documented caveats" rather than Clean, and it is not Caution because retention for API content is explicit rather than vague.
