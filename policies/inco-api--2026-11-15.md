## Inco — research note (2026-11-15)

Pages read: https://inco.ai/legal/terms/ (ToS, effective 2026-10-15), https://inco.ai/legal/privacy/ (privacy policy, effective 2026-10-15), https://inco.ai/legal/dpa/ (DPA), https://platform.inco.ai/models (catalog, operator per model and mode). inco.ai/terms and /privacy 301 to the /legal/ paths. No public status page (`status.inco.ai` does not resolve).

- TRAINING: off by default. "The Training Setting is off by default. While it is on, Inco may use Customer Content to train, fine-tune, or otherwise improve models that Inco releases." (ToS §5.3). The setting can only be on while Store & Improve is on, and is unavailable to European accounts (§5.1). Content logs still feed internal, non-released serving models (draft models, routing/cache tuning) as "Improvement Purposes" while Store & Improve is on (§5.2).
- RETENTION: setting-dependent and unbounded outside the EU. Store & Improve is **on** by default, except for European accounts. "Content Logs of a European Account are deleted no later than 30 days after creation. Content Logs of any other account are retained for as long as necessary for the Improvement Purposes, reviewed at least every 12 months, and deleted when no longer necessary for those purposes." Logs are deleted within 30 days after the setting is switched off and within 30 days of account deletion (§5.11).
- ZDR: self-serve, partial. "While the Store & Improve Setting is off, Inputs and Outputs submitted to or generated by the Service … are retained only for as long as necessary to process the request and return the response" (§5.4). Request metadata, part counts and a one-way fingerprint per part are kept regardless (§5.4, §5.8); IP and user agent 90 days, request metadata for the account's life plus six years, billing ten years (§12).
- RELAY: three of the ten catalog ids — the standard-tier GLM 5.3, GLM 5.3 Flash and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash — are Passthrough Models relayed to Z.ai (Singapore) and DeepSeek (PRC) under those providers' own terms (ToS §4.12; the models page names the operator per id and mode). Every `:fast` id and both MiniMax and Kimi ids are Inco-served. Relayed models are unavailable to European accounts.
- LOCATION: "Inco is based in the United States. The infrastructure we operate to run Inco-served models is located in several regions, which may include North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region … We do not operate inference infrastructure in the People's Republic of China, Taiwan, or Hong Kong." Account, billing and usage metadata is stored in the US (privacy §10). Company address is 1881 Page Mill Road, Palo Alto, California (§15).
- COMPLIANCE: public DPA with EU SCCs and a subprocessor list (§9, Annex 5); no SOC 2 claim; PHI permitted only with prior written agreement (ToS §6.1(f)).
- INCIDENTS: none found.
- Confidence: high (long, explicit policies with versioned effective dates).
- Rating: Caution — unbounded retention outside the EU plus a default-on relay path to third parties, one of which processes in the PRC. Training is off by default, which keeps it out of High Risk.
